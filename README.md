# Trader Behavior vs Market Sentiment Analysis

#Objective
Analyze how market sentiment (Fear/Greed) impacts trader performance, risk-taking, and decision-making.

#Datasets
- Hyperliquid Trader Data  
- Bitcoin Fear & Greed Index
- 
#Approach
- Cleaned and merged datasets using date  
- Created key features:
  - Profit (Closed PnL)  
  - Win Rate  
  - Risk (Size USD as proxy)  
- Performed sentiment-based and trader-level analysis  

# Key Insights
- Traders take **highest risk during Fear**, but performance is not optimal  
- **Extreme Greed shows highest PnL and win rate with lower risk**  
- Top traders take **higher but controlled risk**  
- Losing traders take **lower but inefficient risk**
- 
# Strategy
**Controlled Aggression Strategy**
- Increase participation during strong trends (Extrem  e Greed)  
- Reduce risk during Fear  
- Focus on high-quality, high-conviction trades
  
# Conclusion
Trading success depends on **optimizing risk and adapting to market sentiment**, not avoiding risk entirely.
